S04 continuous-audio detector baseline

137 reference S04 calls · 5 dates · 56 window features.

Fixed logistic regression, 0.8-second windows, 0.2-second steps. Each evaluation recording was scanned by a model trained without its entire recording date.

Exploratory results. Unannotated regions supply background proxies, not verified negatives. Available annotations may omit calls. Event precision/recall are referenced to the available S04 intervals, with greedy one-to-one IoU matching. Scores are uncalibrated.
